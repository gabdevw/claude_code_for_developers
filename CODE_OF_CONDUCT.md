# Code of Conduct

This is a learning space. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience level or language.

## We expect

- Respect and patience, especially with beginners.
- Feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Assuming good intentions.

## We don't accept

- Insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Spam or promotion that adds nothing to the topic.

## What happens if someone breaks these rules

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues or pull requests, and may block people who keep breaking these rules.

To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainers privately through their GitHub profile.
